## App description (long form)

**Be ready for EU Regulation 2023/1115 — enforcement starts December 30, 2026.**

EUDR Compliance Suite is the simplest way to comply with the EU Deforestation
Regulation (EUDR) on Shopify. Classify products, manage production-plot
geolocations, generate Due Diligence Statements (DDS), and display compliance
information on your product pages — all from inside your Shopify admin.

### Why you need an EUDR app

From 30 December 2026 (30 June 2027 for SMEs), every business placing certain
commodities on the EU market — cattle, cocoa, coffee, palm oil, rubber, soya,
or wood (and their derivatives) — must prove the products are deforestation-free.

Non-compliance fines can reach **4 % of EU annual turnover** plus product
seizure and exclusion from public procurement.

### What this app does

- **Classify products in seconds.** Bulk-tag products by vendor or product type,
  assign HS codes, mark in-scope items in a single click. CSV import for full
  catalog migrations.
- **Store geolocation plots.** Add point (≤4 ha) or polygon (>4 ha) production
  plots. Link plots to products. CSV import. Country risk tier auto-fills from
  the EC published list.
- **Generate Due Diligence Statements.** Per-consignment DDS workflow with
  risk assessment, mitigation measures, and EU Information System reference
  tracking. **Bulk DDS generation** for large catalogs (Pro).
- **Publish to your storefront.** One click writes EUDR metafields to your
  Shopify products. Drop the included theme widget on any product page to
  display compliance status, DDS reference, country of production and operator
  details. Multi-language storefront widget — EN, DE, FR, ES, IT — auto-translates
  to customer locale (Pro).
- **PDF export of every DDS** for record-keeping and supplier sharing (Pro).
- **REST API** for ERP / PIM integration (Enterprise).
- **Audit log** of every classification, plot edit, DDS submission and widget
  publish — for legal traceability under Article 4(1).

## Single purpose statement (required by Shopify)
Helps merchants comply with EU Regulation 2023/1115 (EU Deforestation
Regulation) by managing product commodity classifications, geolocation plots,
Due Diligence Statements, and publishing compliance information to their
storefront via product metafields.

## Permission justifications

### read_products
We read product titles, vendors, product types and IDs so merchants can
classify their catalog as in-scope or out-of-scope for EUDR and link products
to commodities and HS codes.

### write_products
We write EUDR compliance metafields (commodity, HS code, country of
production, DDS reference, operator details, compliance statement,
multi-language enabled flag) back to products so the storefront theme app
extension can display them on product pages.

### read_locales
We read the merchant's enabled locales so the storefront widget can render
compliance copy in the correct EU language (EN, DE, FR, ES, IT).
